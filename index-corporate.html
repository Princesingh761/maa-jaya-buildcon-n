About the Firm

Engineering ideas into built, powered, standing structures.

We run construction, solar and industrial engineering under one accountable team — so a project's design, procurement and site execution stay connected instead of passing between disconnected vendors.

Founded in 2023 by Vivek Verma and Nishant Malay, the firm has since delivered residential, commercial, industrial and solar work across Ranchi and Jamshedpur.
